Render on GitHub

Your video is drawn by GitHub’s servers, so it does not depend on your phone.

  • In your own GitHub. It happens in a repo in your own GitHub account, which is public. Your script, voiceover and video are encrypted there.
  • Free. It uses your free GitHub minutes. Nothing is charged.
  • Cleaned up. The finished video is kept for one day and deleted from GitHub as soon as you have downloaded it.
  • Limits. Up to 30 minutes per video. This one uses 1 of your 3 renders today.

You may be asked to connect GitHub once.

Connect GitHub (one time)

Your video is drawn by GitHub on your own free account. Everything private is encrypted, kept for one day and deleted as soon as you have downloaded it.

  1. Tap Create token. Sign in to GitHub if asked.
  2. Scroll down and tap Generate token (the “public_repo” and “workflow” boxes are already ticked).
  3. Copy the token, come back here and paste it below.

Turn an idea into an animated video

This app makes the video for you. You describe it, an AI (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) writes the scenes, and this app turns them into a finished animation you can download for TikTok or YouTube. No drawing and no editing skills needed.
